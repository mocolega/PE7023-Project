\chapter{Introduction}

This report presents the production engineering design for the \emph{New Field} (NF)
subsea development, carried out as part of PE~7023 Advanced Production Engineering.
The field comprises seven production wells tied back to a land-based operations point
(LOP) via a subsea gathering network, and is targeted to deliver a maximum field liquid
rate of 45,000~STB/d over a 30-year production horizon.

The reservoir is a water-flooded system maintained at the bubble-point pressure of
3,800~psia. A declining oil rate combined with an increasing water cut drives the need
for artificial lift, and gas-lift via single-point injection was selected as the only
available option. The work was divided into two parts. Part~1 covers
the engineering design: well sizing using \emph{PIPESIM} nodal analysis
\cite{Schlumberger2023}, subsea pipeline diameter and insulation selection, production
profile construction, and economic evaluation under two oil-price scenarios. Part~2
covers the development of an in-house Python network simulator,
validated against \emph{PIPESIM} results, as an independent tool for production
analysis when commercial software is unavailable.

The complete project description, as issued in the course handout,
is reproduced in \Cref{chap:final_network}.
