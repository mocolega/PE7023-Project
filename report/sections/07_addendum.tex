% ----- Letter 1: Diego Gomez -----------------------------------
\section*{Diego Gomes}

\noindent
\textit{Tulsa, Oklahoma, \today}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
To Whom It May Concern,

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
I, Diego Gomez, hereby confirm my contributions to the Advanced Production Engineering Design Project.

For Part 1 of the project, I was responsible for the Well Design (Section I), which included tubing and casing selection, productivity index analysis, and system modeling using PIPESIM. I also contributed 50\% to the Production Profile and Short Economic Analysis (Section III), including production forecasting and economic evaluation.

For Part 2 of the project, I contributed 50\% of the work, including the development, implementation, and analysis of the required network and wellbore-reservoir simulation codes.

In addition to the technical contributions, I wrote the Executive Summary and was responsible for the overall report design and formatting. Each team member prepared the written sections corresponding to their respective technical contributions.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent
Sincerely,\\[1.5em]
\rule{6cm}{0.4pt}\\
\textbf{Diego Gomez}
